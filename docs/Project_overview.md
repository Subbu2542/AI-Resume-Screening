
## Introduction

The AI Resume Screening & Interview Scheduling System is an intelligent recruitment platform designed to simplify the hiring process.

The application leverages Google's Gemini AI to analyze resumes, compare them against job descriptions, calculate an AI Match Score, and recommend whether a candidate should be shortlisted, manually reviewed, or rejected.

The platform also provides recruiter dashboards, analytics, interview scheduling, and interview email generation.

---

## Objectives

- Reduce manual resume screening
- Improve recruitment efficiency
- Automate candidate evaluation
- Assist recruiters in making hiring decisions
- Generate interview schedules and emails automatically

---

## Major Modules

1. Resume Upload
2. Resume Parsing
3. Candidate Information Extraction
4. Job Description Matching
5. AI Match Score
6. Candidate Recommendation
7. Recruiter Dashboard
8. Recruitment Analytics
9. Interview Scheduler
10. Email Generator
